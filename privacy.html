Privacy Policy

Strategy Arena Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Strategy Arena YouTube channel. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the tool uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and reads that channel's own statistics and comments.

What it stores

Google sign-in tokens are stored privately by the owner, on the owner's computer and in a private project environment. No other user data is collected.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The tool's use of information from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: the channel owner, through the Strategy Arena YouTube channel.
