Strategy Arena

Strategy Arena is a YouTube Shorts channel where game-theory strategies, such as always-cooperate, tit-for-tat and always-betray, compete against each other in a shrinking arena. The videos are generated by a small personal tool.

This page also serves as the homepage for "Strategy Arena Uploader", a personal tool used by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos and read the channel's own analytics. It is not offered to other users.
